# Privacy Policy - Family Finance Iceland

**Last updated: 23 November 2026**

## 1. Purpose

Family Finance Iceland is a personal, non-commercial household financial management application. It is designed to help authorized household members view and analyze their financial information.

## 2. Information Collected

With the account holder's authorization, the application may access bank account details, account balances, transaction dates, amounts, currencies and transaction descriptions through an open banking provider.

## 3. Use of Information

Financial information is used solely for personal household financial management, including tracking income, expenses, balances and spending patterns.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

The application is currently designed to store financial data locally on the user's computer. Bank credentials and private API keys are not published in this repository. Financial information is not intentionally made publicly available.

## 5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell financial data or share it with advertisers. The open banking provider and participating banks may process information as necessary to provide authorized account access.

## 6. Access and Withdrawal

Bank account access requires the account holder's authorization. The account holder may withdraw authorization through the bank or open banking provider. Locally stored data can be deleted separately.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data processing practices change.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application operator using the data protection contact email provided during application registration.
